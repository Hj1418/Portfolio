// about

Engineer by trade, architect by instinct

I'm Harsh — a Backend Engineer based in Pune, India, focused on system architecture and security. I'd rather understand how a system behaves under load than just make sure the button click works.

At NextGen DigiLab, an early-stage startup, I carry more ownership than a typical junior title suggests — designing features, architecting services, integrating AI, and being present in requirement and architecture discussions rather than waiting to be handed a ticket.

My core interests sit at the intersection of scalability, security, and AI: multi-tenant systems that isolate data correctly, authorization that doesn't leak, and LLMs that run locally instead of shipping sensitive data off-premises.

Outside of engineering, I'm usually behind a camera, sketching, deep into a trek, or reading about how companies like Stripe and Cloudflare build for scale.
